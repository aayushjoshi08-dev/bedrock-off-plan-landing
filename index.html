What buyers ask us first

Yes. The off-plan projects we shortlist sit in designated freehold zones, so any nationality — resident or overseas — can own 100% of the property outright, with a title deed registered at the Dubai Land Department.

Property investments of AED 2 million or more can qualify the owner for a 10-year renewable UAE Golden Visa, subject to current eligibility rules. We'll confirm whether the specific unit you shortlist meets the threshold.

You reserve your unit with a 10% down payment paid into a developer escrow account regulated by RERA — funds are released to the developer only against construction milestones. The remaining balance follows a structured plan across the build, so you never pay everything upfront.

No. The UAE levies no annual property tax and no personal income tax on rental earnings. A one-time DLD registration fee (typically 4%) applies at purchase. This tax-free environment is a core reason yields here outperform London, Mumbai or Singapore.

Prime UAE communities typically deliver 6–8% gross rental yields, plus capital appreciation between purchase and handover. We build a unit-specific model with you — factoring service charges and current rental trends — so the numbers are realistic, not marketing.

All transactions are in AED, which is pegged to the US dollar for currency stability. Non-resident mortgages are available through several UAE banks, usually up to 50–60% loan-to-value. For off-plan, many buyers prefer the developer's interest-free construction payment plan instead.

Off-plan property is a home you buy from the developer before or during construction. You lock in today's launch price, pay a booking amount (often 10–20%), and pay the rest in instalments during the build and at handover — instead of paying the full price upfront like a ready property.

Some developers let you keep paying part of the price after you receive the keys, spread over a set period. It lowers the cash you need before handover — and if you rent the unit out, rent can help cover the remaining instalments. We compare payment plans across projects so the one you choose fits your finances.
